## Sample 1 — A5 everyday journal

**Illustrative buyer brief:** Fictional brand; stitched A5 journal; 120 ruled pages; lay-flat binding; 80gsm paper; reusable elastic closure. All specs here are invented for a sample and must not be used for a real item without validation.

**Title:** A5 Lay-Flat Journal — Your Everyday Ideas, All in One Place

**Description (74 words):**

Keep plans, sketches and daily thoughts together in a journal that opens flat when inspiration strikes. This A5 notebook has 120 ruled pages of 80gsm paper, a stitched binding and a simple elastic closure that helps hold loose notes in place. The portable format slips into a work bag or backpack, ready for quick meeting notes, lists or evening reflection. Pick your favourite cover and make a little space for your next great idea.

**Highlights:** A5 portable format; 120 ruled pages; stitched lay-flat binding; elastic closure.

**Suggested SEO title:** A5 Lay-Flat Ruled Journal | 120-Page Notebook

**Suggested meta description:** Capture plans and everyday ideas with a stitched A5 lay-flat journal, 120 ruled pages and a practical elastic closure.

**Editorial rules:** No claims that the paper is recyclable or sourced sustainably without written evidence; do not claim waterproofing, tear-resistance or fountain-pen compatibility without tests.

## Sample 2 — Insulated travel mug

**Illustrative buyer brief:** Fictional stainless-steel double-wall travel mug, 350 ml, screw-on spill-resistant lid, no handle, hand-wash recommended. Specs are fictional demonstration data.

**Title:** 350 ml Insulated Travel Mug — A Simple Sip on the Move

**Description (72 words):**

From early train journeys to desk-side coffee breaks, this compact 350 ml travel mug helps make your favourite drink part of the day. Its double-wall stainless-steel body feels sturdy in hand, while the screw-on lid is designed to reduce everyday splashes when you're heading between stops. The clean, handle-free shape is easy to pack with your daily essentials. Hand-wash after use and enjoy a reliable companion for busy mornings, commutes and small adventures.

**Highlights:** 350 ml capacity; double-wall stainless-steel design; screw-on, spill-resistant lid; compact, handle-free shape.

**Suggested SEO title:** 350 ml Double-Wall Stainless-Steel Travel Mug

**Suggested meta description:** Take coffee or tea on the move with a compact 350 ml stainless-steel travel mug, double-wall body and screw-on splash-resistant lid.

**Editorial rules:** Do not say 'leakproof', 'dishwasher safe', 'keeps drinks hot for 12 hours', or 'BPA-free' without manufacturer proof. 'Spill-resistant' is used only because the fictional brief states that feature.
