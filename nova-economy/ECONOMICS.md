# NOVA Economic Design — Initial Specification

NOVA is designed as an ecosystem utility/credit asset, not a promise of profit
or an investment product.

Potential utility, subject to product and legal review:
- API/service credits
- Factory marketplace payments
- Developer/service-provider settlement
- Product access where appropriate

Supply and distribution remain configuration decisions. Model ecosystem utility,
treasury/operations, development, partnerships, and any liquidity separately,
with documented ownership, purpose, vesting/unlock rules, and accounting.

The contract has an immutable maximum supply, capped minting, and an emergency
pause. It has no transfer fee, blacklist, arbitrary balance seizure, automatic
market making, or automated public sale.

Factory revenue must continue to be recognized only from verified commercial
transactions. Token holdings or market value are not revenue.

Before any public launch: select jurisdiction and chain, review applicable
financial/crypto, consumer, tax, AML/KYC, sanctions, privacy and marketing
requirements; obtain a security review; test on a test network; verify source;
and explicitly authorize any production deployment.
