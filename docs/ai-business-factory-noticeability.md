# AI Business Factory: Noticeability & Distribution Research

Updated: 2026-09-19

AI Business Factory is being built around a simple constraint: software can be generated faster than attention, trust and distribution can be earned.

## Observed operating reality

The factory currently publishes a live product, a research library, a founder profile, machine-readable product information and a free AI audit. Internal telemetry is treated as first-party evidence only.

## Current distribution framework

1. **Specificity** — one buyer, one painful workflow, one clear outcome.
2. **Useful work** — publish original frameworks, research, tools and transparent benchmarks.
3. **Founder presence** — participate as a person in relevant communities rather than behaving like a broadcast bot.
4. **Discoverability** — maintain crawlable pages, structured information, internal links and legitimate referring sources.
5. **Credibility** — separate external evidence, hypotheses and first-party observations.
6. **Conversion** — every visibility asset should lead to a useful action such as trying a product, requesting an audit or starting a real conversation.
7. **Measurement** — prefer qualified visits, activations, conversations, retention, referrals and verified revenue over raw impressions.
