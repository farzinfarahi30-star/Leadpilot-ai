## Accounting rules

- A completed service is a distinct, specific, no-charge deliverable containing actionable findings/replacement copy, actually sent to a real business through connected Gmail.
- The 24 previously sent free-service OFFERS remain separate and are not credited.
- Prospect discoveries, draft messages, public forum requests, bounce/auto acknowledgments, and paid offers are not deliveries.
- Repeat work for an already counted business is not a new unique business under this ledger.
- Before counting additional sends, search Gmail Sent, suppressions/opt-outs and verify that the recipient explicitly requested constructive feedback.
- Sending does not mean the recipient read the content or became a customer; this project target is free-service delivery count.
