## Decision protocol
First-party dated evidence + real company/domain + problem + current deadline + authorization to respond + no duplicate in Gmail + NOVA can honestly fulfill = eligible candidate. Public feedback requests never imply consent to cold email or budget. Do not bypass blocked email provider sends. Never invent expertise, previous clients, company formation or tax records.

## Income definitions
Source leads: evidence of need. Free review SENT: sent item but no acknowledgement. Sales conversation: real human buyer response. Signed scope: genuine accepted terms. Revenue: processor-confirmed paid payment, not open checkouts, points, shares, or estimates.
