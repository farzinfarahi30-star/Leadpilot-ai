## What the workflow checks

Typical work includes exact duplicates, missing values, obvious formatting inconsistencies, header cleanup, SKU/key consistency, row-width issues, basic email-format flags, catalogue changes, source/target reconciliation, schema checks and concise QA reporting.

Ambiguous values are flagged rather than guessed. The original source is preserved where practical.

## Supplier Price Change Checker

The Nova Supplier Price Change Checker compares old and new CSV price-list exports locally in the browser. It can identify:

- new and removed keys/SKUs
- price increases and decreases
- duplicate keys
- price deltas
- exportable change reports

For XLSX files, export the relevant worksheet to CSV before using the local checker.
