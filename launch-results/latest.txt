Frequently asked questions
Is it free to submit my product?

Yes. Submitting a product to Product Finder is completely free. Every submission goes through a manual quality review before it is published.

How long does the review take?

Free submissions join the standard review queue, which takes 3-4 months due to the volume of submissions. If you don't want to wait, you can get a priority review for a one-time $9.99 fee and your product goes live within 24 hours.

What can I submit?

Products, tools, SaaS, mobile and desktop apps, browser extensions, and add-ons. Anything with a live website can be submitted.

Do I get a backlink to my website?

Every published product gets its own listing page on 10015.io. Links on free listings are nofollow; featured products (from $2.99/month) get a Google-indexed product page and a dofollow backlink from a high-DA domain.

How can I get more visibility after my product is listed?

You can feature your product to get top placement on the Product Finder home page, category and tag pages, a featured badge, and SEO benefits like Google indexing and a dofollow backlink.

Can I update my product information later?

Yes. Reach out to contact@10015.io with your changes and we'll update your listing.
