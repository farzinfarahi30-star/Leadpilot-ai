## Worked mathematics

For integer x,y, equation x²+y²=3 has no integer solutions: modulo 4 every square is 0 or 1; sums are 0, 1 or 2, never 3. This example is a classical known fact and not new research. The included `modular_obstruction.py` finds a finite modulus and emits a certificate with every residue combination checked; `verify_certificate` recomputes the check independently. A failed search prints INCONCLUSIVE and must never be interpreted as existence.
