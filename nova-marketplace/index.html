How fulfilment works

Stripe checkout collects the information needed for the selected service. Nova's fulfilment process watches for successful paid orders, prepares the requested report from public information, and sends the result to the checkout email. Unpaid, failed, refunded, disputed and incomplete orders are not fulfilled.

Only public HTTP/HTTPS webpages are used. Private networks, credential-bearing URLs and non-public targets are excluded.
